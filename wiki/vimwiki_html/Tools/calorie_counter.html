The arithmetic

Every number here is a population average fitted to your height, weight and age. Real resting metabolic rates vary by roughly 200 kcal either side of the estimate, food values assume typical home portions, and the activity rates come from published MET tables that describe averages, not you. Treat the ledger as a starting hypothesis: weigh yourself under the same conditions for two or three weeks, and where the scale disagrees with the prediction, the scale is right.

This is an estimation tool, not medical or dietary advice. It cannot account for medication, thyroid or metabolic conditions, pregnancy, injury, or any of the reasons a real body departs from a population average. If you are planning a large change to how you eat or train, or you have a health condition, talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian first. It is not designed for anyone under 18, and it should not be used to drive weight loss in anyone with a history of disordered eating.

Your data never leaves your device. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no network request except the web font, which the page works fine without. Profiles live in this browser's local storage, so clearing site data removes them — use Export to keep a copy.
